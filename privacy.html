Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Connexa GmbH, Switzerland

Realm is a game in which you claim territory on a world map by drawing loops with your real-world location. This policy explains which data the app uses and why. We collect as little as possible: Realm has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Location

Realm uses your precise location only while the map is open and during a round you start (a round lasts at most 60 minutes and may continue while your iPhone is locked; iOS shows a location indicator). Your route is used on your device to detect when you close a loop. Only the outline of a loop you close (your "territory") is uploaded. Your full route is not uploaded.

What other players can see

Claimed territories are public game content. They are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database) together with your territory color, the time it was claimed, an anonymous player identifier issued by iCloud, and your Game Center display name (or a generated name such as "Explorer 4F2A" if you are not signed in to Game Center). All players can see territories on the map. Because territories reflect real places, we recommend not starting rounds at home if you don't want others to infer where you live.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your territory size and coins earned are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Progress

Coins, colors, statistics and blocked players are stored on your device and synced with your own iCloud key-value storage, which only you can access.

Reports

If you report a player, we receive the reported player's identifier and name, the territory in question, the reason you selected, and your anonymous player identifier. We use reports only to moderate the game.

Notifications

Realm only uses local notifications (for example "5 minutes left"). No push notification tokens are collected.

No tracking, no selling

We do not track you across apps or websites, we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we never sell or share data with third parties. Data is processed by Apple as our infrastructure provider (iCloud, Game Center).

Retention and deletion

Territories remain in the game until you delete them. You can delete all territories you claimed and reset all progress at any time in the app under Profile › Delete All My Data. Deleting the app removes local data. You can also contact us to request deletion.

Children

Realm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. Switzerland, EU/EEA, California) you have rights to access, correct and delete your data and to object to processing. Contact us to exercise them.
