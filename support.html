How to play

Start a round

Tap Start Round. You have 60 minutes. Realm records your route while you move – also when your iPhone is locked.

Close a loop

Return to your start point, or back into your own territory. Everything inside your route becomes yours. A loop needs at least 100 m².

Any way you like

Walk, run, cycle, sail – or ride as a passenger on a train, bus or plane. Never use your phone while driving: Realm claims land automatically, there's nothing to tap while you move.

Rivals

The most recent loop always wins. Draw a loop over a rival's land and it becomes yours – and they can take it back.

Coins

Every day you hold land, it earns 1 coin per 1,000 m² (at least 1 coin). Collect up to 7 days at once in the app. Coins unlock territory colors. Coins cannot be bought and have no monetary value.

Your data

Realm has no accounts. You can delete every territory you claimed and reset all progress at any time in Profile › Delete All My Data. See the Privacy Policy.
